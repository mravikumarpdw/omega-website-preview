Our Story

Built on grit. Driven by precision.

Omega Construction Works is a civil engineering and construction contractor based in Kalaburagi, Karnataka, led by partners K Vijay Raju and M Ravi Kumar. We take on the work that keeps a region moving — water conveyed across kilometres of terrain, roads that carry commerce, canals that feed farmland and buildings that house people and industry.

From large-diameter MS pipe laying for lift irrigation to roads, canal lining and structures, we bring the same discipline to every site: careful planning, safe execution, honest measurement and on-time delivery.
